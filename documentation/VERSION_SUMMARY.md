## How to Update This File

- **Add a row when a feature is shippable**, not when work starts. Work-in-progress belongs in
  `DAILY_CHANGES.md`.
- **Newest version section at the top**; newest row at the top of its section.
- **Describe outcomes in plain language** — what a user or partner can now do, and why it matters.
  Not "added `PartnerService`".
- **Bold the feature name** for substantial work; leave it plain for small changes.
- **Status:** ✅ Completed · 🔄 In Progress · ⏸️ On Hold · ⚠️ Inherited
- **Open a new version section** when a release boundary is agreed, with its date range in the heading.
